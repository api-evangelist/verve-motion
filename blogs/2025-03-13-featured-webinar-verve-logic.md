Verve Logic™, Verve Motion’s dedicated software platform for data analytics, helps users gain actionable insights into lifting safety at their facilities, from individual employees to company-wide reports. The real-time safety and productivity data captured by Verve Motion’s SafeLift™ exosuit help facilities avoid employee injuries, track key performance metrics, and identify high-impact teams and scenarios for prioritized exosuit distribution – all within the Verve Logic user interface.
