Verve Motion’s SafeLift™ exosuit is a cutting-edge wearable robot that reduces lower back strain by up to 40% for workers engaged in repetitive lifting and lowering tasks. As a result of decreasing the amount of fatigue users feel over the course of their shifts, SafeLift reduces the rate of back injuries by up to 85% and boosts facility productivity by as much as 8%. SafeLift is powered by a pair of lightweight, high-power density lithium-ion batteries, designed to meet the demands of repetitive lifting and lowering work.
