In physically demanding industries like warehousing, distribution, and logistics, protecting workers from injury is a top priority — especially those operating at height. Traditionally, fall harnesses have played a critical role in these environments, helping protect against fall-related injuries. Worn securely around the torso, shoulders, and legs, and connected via a D-ring to a lanyard or lifeline, these systems are designed to catch a worker mid-fall, distribute impact forces across their body, and keep them upright for safe rescue.
