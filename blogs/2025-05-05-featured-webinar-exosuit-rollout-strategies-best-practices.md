Determining the best way to implement exosuits in a distribution center can be challenging. In this webinar, we walk through four key deployment scenarios to help you choose the right approach for your team. From using exosuits to support new hires to full-site rollouts, we cover the rationale behind each deployment program, the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to track success, and best practices for customer roles and responsibilities.
