# Verve Motion

> Verve Motion is a wearable robotics company out of the Harvard Biodesign Lab. Its product is SafeLift, a soft powered exosuit worn by industrial workers who lift repetitively: inertial sensors woven into the suit detect the lift and trigger assistive force to the wearer's back. Verve Logic, the companion cloud platform, collects the movement data the suit produces and reports ergonomic risk, unsafe-posture trends and exosuit utilization to safety managers. SafeLift is sold as a subscription that bundles the exosuit, the Verve Logic portal and Verve Care support.
